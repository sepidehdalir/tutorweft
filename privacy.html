Privacy, with a clear record.

Tutorweft is a local lesson-package and manual-payment tracker by Sepideh Javanbakht Dalir / Arvinify. The app does not collect your data for the developer and has no developer account system, backend, advertising, analytics or tracking.

Records stay on your device

Students, contact or payer details, private notes, lesson appointments, attendance history, package credits, quoted charges, payments, refunds and teacher profile details are stored in the app’s local library. They are not transmitted to a developer server.

Your selected teacher logo

The app uses Apple’s photo picker to access only an image you select for teacher branding. It processes the selected image locally into a smaller logo copy. It does not scan or upload your photo library. A logo may be included in a branded document or backup you choose to export.

Exports and backups are your choice

PDFs, payment CSV, calendar files and library backups are created on the device. You choose whether and where to save or share them through Apple’s sharing interface. A chosen destination may be a person, cloud-storage service or another app, governed by that recipient’s privacy practices. Check the recipient and document preview before sharing.

Private student notes are excluded from shared PDFs and calendar exports. Lesson notes are excluded from package statements unless you explicitly select their inclusion. Internal CSV and library backups contain private records. Keep them in a location you control.

Local reminders

Notification permission is optional and requested only when you enable a reminder. Notifications are scheduled locally and use generic text without student names or lesson details. iOS permission, Focus settings and scheduling limits apply. The app does not send remote push notifications.

Apple purchases and device backups

Apple manages Annual Pro and Lifetime Pro purchases, billing and restoration. Tutorweft checks Apple-verified transactions to determine access; it does not receive your payment-card details or operate its own purchase server. Apple may process purchase information under its policies.

Device or iCloud backups depend on your Apple settings and may include the local app library. Tutorweft does not provide its own cloud sync or cloud account. Restore Purchases restores purchase access, not student data.

Retention and removal

Archived students retain history. Trash is recoverable and is not permanent deletion. Removing the app removes its local library and files inside the app container. Exported files and device backups outside the app are managed separately by you and the destination service. Keep an external backup before removing the app if you want to retain your records.

Support

If you email support, the developer processes the message and details you choose to send in order to respond. This is separate from the app’s local record workflow. Do not send credentials, payment-card details or private student records. When possible, describe an issue using fictional examples.
