Workflow & support

Keep the teaching thread.

Start with a student and package

In Students, add a name and optional subject, contact or payer details and private notes. Open the student and add a package: choose a session count, quoted amount, currency, cancellation policy and optional expiry. Each package keeps its own original policy and currency. Enter decimal amounts using a dot. Currency conversion is not provided.

Sessions and money are separate

A new package grants its original sessions. Scheduling does not deduct them. A completed lesson uses one session; excused and teacher-cancelled lessons use none. Student cancellations and no-shows follow the package’s saved policy. Repeating the same outcome does not consume twice. Correct an outcome with a reason; changing between charged outcomes keeps one deduction. A correction to an uncharged outcome returns its prior credit.

Record money actually received in Record payment or refund. A refund cannot exceed the package’s prior net received amount. Payments do not consume sessions. Charge adjustments change the quoted amount owed with a separate reason; they do not move money. Different currencies are never combined.

Reschedule, adjust and roll over

Rescheduling keeps the same lesson identity and reopens it as scheduled. If it had consumed a credit, that credit returns once; completing the lesson later consumes it again. The original recurring-series anchor is preserved. Use explicit credit/debit adjustments with a reason. Remaining sessions cannot become negative.

Rollover creates a new package for the same student and currency and writes linked entries on both packages. Select how many unused sessions to move and explicitly enter any additional new sessions and quoted amount. Old payments and outstanding charges remain with the old package. Expiry does not erase history or unused credits; extend it or roll unused sessions forward explicitly.

Schedule a finite series with Pro

Enable Create a finite recurring series, choose selected weekdays or a day interval, set the count and inspect the date preview before saving. A series contains at most 104 lessons and does not renew or sell a package. Its time zone is saved, and local wall-clock scheduling follows calendar daylight-saving rules. A series extending beyond package expiry is rejected until you reduce its count or extend expiry. Existing lessons remain readable if Pro ends.

Share a statement or receipt

Open a package and choose Preview statement PDF. Select A4 or US Letter, inspect every page, then use Share to save to Files or send it to your chosen destination. Private student notes stay off the PDF. Lesson notes are excluded unless you explicitly include them. A payment receipt uses the teacher profile saved when that payment was recorded. Pro enables teacher branding; set your details in Settings.

Statements and receipts acknowledge manually entered records. Tutorweft does not process payments, verify bank settlement or produce tax invoices.

Back up before you need it

In Settings, choose Export library backup and save the .tutorweft file outside the app. Backups contain private records and any saved logo. Import through Settings or open the backup file with Tutorweft. Review the import: records receive fresh identities and package numbers while existing work stays intact. Importing twice creates two copies. Imported payment records count as new records in reports.

Free permits three active students. Choose to import students as archived when a larger backup would exceed that allowance. Decide explicitly whether to import teacher defaults. Restore Purchases restores Pro access, not the lesson library. Removing the app removes its local data; retain an external backup.

Archive, Trash and recovery

Archived students keep their history and do not use the active-student allowance. Archived students are excluded from upcoming lessons and reminders. Enable archived students in reports to include their packages. Restoring a student checks the active allowance.

Moving a lesson to Trash returns any consumed session with a reasoned history. Restoring it does not automatically consume a session; record an outcome again when appropriate. Only unused packages without lessons, payments or adjustments can move to Trash. No automatic purge occurs.

If a save fails, the prior library remains intact and an error is shown. Unreadable libraries are preserved rather than silently reset. Recover with a valid backup after explicit confirmation; a separate copy of the unreadable original is kept. Invalid or unsupported backups are rejected. Backup and local library file limits protect safe loading.

Reminders and calendar files

Enable reminders on individual lessons and allow notifications when requested. The nearest 50 eligible future scheduled lessons for active students are scheduled with generic text. Past reminder times are excluded. iOS settings and Focus can suppress delivery. Reopen the app after data recovery or a time-zone change.

Pro calendar export includes selected scheduled lessons for active students. It creates an .ics file you choose to share. No calendar permission, automatic sync or ongoing link is created. Importing the same file repeatedly in another calendar app may create duplicates.

Income, CSV and Pro access

Cash summaries group recorded payments minus refunds by payment month and currency, including archived students. They do not treat a package sale as cash received. Free shows the current month; Pro adds history and complete internal payment CSV. CSV includes private records and is not a customer receipt.

The app displays Apple’s localized prices. Annual Pro renews unless cancelled through Apple subscriptions. Lifetime Pro is a separate one-time purchase and does not cancel an annual subscription. Use Restore Purchases with the same Apple account. If prices fail to load, reconnect and retry; do not purchase from a blank or unexpected offer. Pending purchases unlock only after Apple returns a verified entitlement. Saved records remain available if Pro ends; Pro creation/export features require access.
